About United Charter School
Gujrat

Building academic confidence, holistic character, and curiosity through a supportive and future-focused learning environment.

UCS City Campus Gujrat is committed to providing an engaging learning environment, modern learning resources, and a welcoming space where students can discover their strengths and prepare for the future.

Message from the Principal

Ms. Aisha Agnesia

PRINCIPAL, CITY CAMPUS GUJRAT

“At City Campus Gujrat, we believe that every child arrives with unique potential. Our responsibility is to create an environment where that potential can flourish through thoughtful teaching, meaningful experiences, and genuine care.”

Our academic approach encourages curiosity, resilience, and independent thinking. Alongside classroom learning, we value creativity, physical wellbeing, digital awareness, and the development of strong personal values.

We know that children thrive when school and home work together. Our team aims to build respectful partnerships with families and provide a warm, inclusive, and student-focused atmosphere where each learner feels supported.

As we continue to grow, our goal remains clear: to help students become capable learners, compassionate citizens, and confident contributors to their communities.

“Welcome to UCS Gujrat — where every child's journey matters.”
