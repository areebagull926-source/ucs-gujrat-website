Privacy Policy

United Charter School Gujrat • City Campus

We respect the privacy of students, parents, guardians, and visitors. This page explains how information submitted through the sample website may be handled.

01Information You Submit

The admission form requests a student's name, chosen programme, date of birth, and contact phone number. Please provide only accurate information necessary for the enquiry.

02How Information Is Used

Information submitted through the admission form is intended to help the school respond to admission enquiries and follow up about the selected programme.

03Form Service & Email

The demonstration form uses FormSubmit to deliver submissions to the configured school email. The first submission may require email activation by the recipient. Review the service's own privacy terms before using it for real student data.

04Data Safety & Retention

Access to submitted details should be limited to authorised school personnel. Do not publish or share a student's personal information unnecessarily. A production website should use a verified, school-approved form provider and documented retention practices.

05Questions

For questions about an admission enquiry or your information, contact the school through its official communication channels.
